    How to play

    Each day there is one mystery MRT station. Type a station, pick it from the list and press Enter. Each guess adds a row of hints:

    • Line – the guessed station's line(s).
    • Structure – underground, above ground, or both (interchanges).
    • Distance – straight-line km to the mystery station, with an arrow pointing towards it.
    • Stops away – fewest stations to travel to reach it, switching lines allowed.
